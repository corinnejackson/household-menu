Stage Breakfast & Texture Rules

Stage 1 (6–7m): Smooth Puree & Thinned Oats Unlocks Stage 1 breakfasts. Thinned infant oat cereal, purees, thinned peanut/almond butter, soft egg yolk.
Stage 2 (8–9m): Thick Porridge & Soft Finger Strips Unlocks Stage 1 & 2 breakfasts. Whole fluffy scrambled eggs, soft banana spears, chia/hemp yogurts.
Stage 3 (10–12m): Pancake Bites & Chopped Fruit Unlocks Stages 1–3. Baby oat/banana finger pancakes, chopped berries, toast strips with thin nut butter.
Stage 4 (12m+): Family Breakfast & Cow's Milk All breakfasts unlocked. Regular rolled oats, whole cow's milk, honey allowed as natural sweetener.
